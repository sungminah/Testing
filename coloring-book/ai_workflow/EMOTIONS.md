## Phân công cho cảnh 6 đến 10

Có 11 cảm xúc chưa dùng. Mỗi trang cần 6 cảm xúc (5 hamster và chú lữ khách), nên từ trang thứ 4 trở đi bắt đầu lặp lại một ít, nhưng luôn ở con khác và trang khác.

### Cảnh 6: Thi nấu ăn: quán ramen và sân thi nấu

| Con | Cảm xúc | Công thức |
|---|---|---|
| Hamster 1 | Tức giận | eyebrows slanted sharply down; eyes as small empty circles under the brows; mouth a wavy clenched line showing a tooth line; two anger marks |
| Hamster 2 | Hoảng hốt | eyebrows high and tilted; eyes as tiny empty circles; mouth wide open in a wobbly oval; four sweat drops flying outward |
| Hamster 3 | Thanh thản | soft eyebrows; eyes as small empty ovals with a flat lid line across the top; small calm smile; a small leaf outline floating nearby |
| Hamster 4 | Tinh nghịch | one eyebrow raised; eyes as one small empty circle and one smaller empty circle; tongue sticking out; small shine mark |
| Hamster 5 | Cảm động | eyebrows tilted upward; eyes as small empty circles; mouth a small wobbly smile; two tear drops and two sparkle marks |
| Chú lữ khách | Ngượng ngùng | eyebrows tilted; eyes as small empty circles looking down; mouth a tiny crooked smile; one sweat drop; a hand scratching the back of the head |

Khối dán vào prompt (dán sau phần Characters, thay cho mọi mô tả cảm xúc khác):

```
EMOTIONS: every hamster has a DIFFERENT emotion, one per hamster, and each emotion must clearly differ from the others on this page. Assign them to the hamsters in the way that fits what each one is doing. Keep every face simple: eyes are empty outlines only (never filled black, no pupils, no highlight dots), no blush, no gray.
- Hamster 1: TỨC GIẬN. eyebrows slanted sharply down; eyes as small empty circles under the brows; mouth a wavy clenched line showing a tooth line; two anger marks.
- Hamster 2: HOẢNG HỐT. eyebrows high and tilted; eyes as tiny empty circles; mouth wide open in a wobbly oval; four sweat drops flying outward.
- Hamster 3: THANH THẢN. soft eyebrows; eyes as small empty ovals with a flat lid line across the top; small calm smile; a small leaf outline floating nearby.
- Hamster 4: TINH NGHỊCH. one eyebrow raised; eyes as one small empty circle and one smaller empty circle; tongue sticking out; small shine mark.
- Hamster 5: CẢM ĐỘNG. eyebrows tilted upward; eyes as small empty circles; mouth a small wobbly smile; two tear drops and two sparkle marks.
- The traveler hamster in front: NGƯỢNG NGÙNG. eyebrows tilted; eyes as small empty circles looking down; mouth a tiny crooked smile; one sweat drop; a hand scratching the back of the head.
```
